Winona State runs a pair of Purple Air PM 2.5 air quality sensors.  
Smoke of particle size 2.5um and smaller is especially dangerous!  
Here's a letter to the editor that one of the department professors wrote a few summers ago.  
https://ntmoore.github.io/notes/strib_PM2.5.html

## 2026-October-20
Cool fact of the day: A 9v battery is 6x 1.5v batteries, wired up "head to tail".  
If you have a wire cutter and a dead 9v, this is pretty easy to see for yourself!  
Is a 9v just 6x AAA's?  
Nope, I think they're actually made from 4A or AAAA batteries! 
![A 9v battery is 6 1.5v batteries, wired in series](10-20_9v.jpg)
![A 9v battery is made of AAAA batteries, which are slightly smaller than AAA size](10-20AAAA.jpg)

## 2026-October-20
Summer Memories: in June at around the time of summer registration, the Saskatoons 
aka Serviceberries aka Shadbush aka Juneberries are ripe outside of the Physics and 
Geoscience Professors' office windows.  
Why so many different names?  The berries are so common in Saskatchewan that they 
named a town after them, or, the berries flower when Shad run up the rivers in the 
North-east US, or, the fruit is ready in June, or, the plants flower at about the 
time the ground thaws enough to have funeral services for all the people who died 
over the winter!  Yikes!, but then again, maybe it is fun to know all of the different 
names for a plant that's all over the northern US. 
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amelanchier

Unbelievable and amazing fact of the day.  Each section of lichen growing on this 
rock is a hardy civilization of fungi and algae cells.  Lichen "eats" rock, and is 
so hardy that small colonies have been found growing on the OUTSIDE of the International Space Station!
