## 2026-October-29
Here's a very red sunrise over Lake Superior.  The red haze comes from wildfire smoke.  The sun takes up about 0.52 degrees of the horizon.  If you look at the picture, you might believe that the sun is about "3 suns" above the horizon.  You can measure stuff like this with ImageJ.  I did that and saw that in this picture, the sun is about 229 pixels in size, and the bottom of the sun is about 645 pixels above the Lake Superior horizon.
So, if each sun diameter is 0.52 degrees, this means the sun is (645/229)*0.52 = 1.46 degrees above the horizon.  The sun moves 360 degrees through the sky each day, which means this picture was taken (1.46/360)*24hrs = 0.097 hours = 5.8 minutes after sunrise.  If you like nerding out over photo measurements, you'll like our department!     
Here's a web version of ImageJ https://ij.imjoy.io

## 2026-October-25
Here are the cheapest 2x4's at my local lumberyard - typically at least $1 less per board because they're warped or checked.  Knowing the required tolerances in a backyard project is a nice thing.  Does it matter if the 2x4 you're using for a canoe rack is 1/2" out of true?  Nope, the canoe won't care and spending less money is fun.  A substantial part of any Engineering design is using fewer, cheaper, or "lower quality" materials to meet a specification.  If you meet the specification a lower cost, you can use less material, and sell the product for a more affordable price.  Engineering design is fun!

## 2026-October-20
Cool fact of the day: A 9v battery is 6x 1.5v batteries, wired up "head to tail".  
If you have a wire cutter and a dead 9v, this is pretty easy to see for yourself!  
Is a 9v just 6x AAA's?  
Nope, I think they're actually made from 4A or AAAA batteries!

## 2026-October-20
Summer Memories: in June at around the time of summer registration, the Saskatoons 
aka Serviceberries aka Shadbush aka Juneberries are ripe outside of the Physics and 
Geoscience Professors' office windows.  
Why so many different names?  The berries are so common in Saskatchewan that they 
named a town after them, or, the berries flower when Shad run up the rivers in the 
North-east US, or, the fruit is ready in June, or, the plants flower at about the 
time the ground thaws enough to have funeral services for all the people who died 
over the winter!  Yikes!, but then again, maybe it is fun to know all of the different 
names for a plant that's all over the northern US.
